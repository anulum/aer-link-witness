# Data and authority boundaries

## Present tooling

Maintained source, selected executable files and the locked tooling environment are inputs to repository controls. Received descriptors and generated inventories cannot choose policy or execute traffic. Checks inspect bounded local scopes and report fixed diagnostics. Byte identity is separate from source interpretation and physical qualification.

An inventory is not a concurrent filesystem snapshot or power-failure durability guarantee. Native children use maintained finite argument vectors and direct-child deadlines; completed-output limits are not live volume ceilings. Foundation qualification is in progress.

## Future schedule and observation inputs

Plans, source records, raw traffic, clock profiles and reports are supplied data. They do not grant capture, program execution, signing, equipment, provider, spend or publication authority. Independent source, raw decoder and actual instrument admission precede their use.

Preserve complete payload and identity, original raw order, clock reset/wrap mapping, coverage gaps, pending work and every prior population cell. No silent cropping, invented receipt, negative loss, erased first failure, merged simulation/fabric scope or fabricated service bound is admissible.

Unknown source, adapter, clock, coverage, capture or current trust cannot become positive acceptance. A signature does not establish instrument independence or link correctness. Shared evidence, equipment and operational recovery remain separate unimplemented domain boundaries; no isolation, physical, scientific or durability guarantee is claimed here.
